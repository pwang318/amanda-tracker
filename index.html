Contract & Closing CallsListing agent, buyer agent, and clear-to-close 3 scripts
Under Contract Call (Listing Agent)
Call the listing agent as soon as a deal you're the buyer's lender on goes under contract. Set the tone early — this is your first real touchpoint with them on this file.
"Hey [Agent Name], this is [Your Name] from the Daniel Sa Team at NFM Lending. I'm calling about your listing at [Property Address], which has just gone into contract. I'm the lender representing the buyer. First, I have a couple of questions about the transaction, I want to make sure we have a smooth process. Does that sound good? Congratulations again on the sale."
Questions to cover:
  • Any special accommodations for your seller, a POA or mail-away closing, we should know about?
  • Will your seller use the sale proceeds to purchase another home?
  • Will this be a simultaneous closing?
    • Would it help to get the final closing package and closing disclosure three full days in advance?
    • Do you know which title company will handle your seller's next purchase?
  • Do you have a transaction coordinator we should keep updated throughout?
"Our team is available nights and weekends, so I'm going to send you a quick text with my contact info. Please don't hesitate to reach out anytime if you or your seller need anything."
Close for the meeting before ending the call.
After the call, send a follow-up text with your contact info.
Under Contract Call (Buyer's Agent)
Call the buyer's agent once the file goes under contract. This call sets the tone for how you will work together throughout.
"Hey [Agent Name], this is [Your Name] from the Daniel Sa Team at NFM Lending. I'm calling about [Buyer Name]'s contract on [Property Address] — congratulations to you and your buyers for going into contract! I wanted to reach out personally and introduce myself. I'll be your main point of contact throughout the transaction and our team is here to support you every step of the way."
Questions to cover:
  • Are there any concerns or anything we should know going in?
  • What's the best way to keep you updated throughout the process — text, email, calls?
  • Regarding the closing date — we want to make sure we stick with the contracted date. We do not move up closing dates. Once we are clear to close, if all parties are in agreement we can revisit at that point.
"Our team is available nights and weekends, so I'm going to send you a quick text with my contact info. Please reach out anytime. We are going to take good care of your buyer."
If they don't already work with us regularly, close for a meeting before ending the call: "I'd love to sit down sometime, even just 15 minutes, to see if it makes sense to work together."
After the call, send a follow-up text with your contact info.
CTC Call
Process: call the agent, leave a voicemail if no answer, follow up with a text if still no response.
"Hi [Agent Name], this is [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM Lending. Congrats on getting [Buyer/Property] to clear to close! I wanted to check in personally, was there anything that went really smoothly for you, or anything we could've done better on our end?"
If it's the listing agent and they don't already work with us, bridge straight into the meeting ask before ending the call:

Cold Call — Opening Script
Before you dial: Pull up RETR and look them up. Find a recent sale or listing and mention it in your opener — "I saw your listing at [address] just closed, congrats!" It makes the call personal and shows you did your homework.
Goal: introduce yourself, explain how we support agents, and schedule a 20-minute meeting.
"Hey [Agent Name], I wanted to reach out and introduce myself. We love working alongside agents to help buyers better understand their financing options, create listing-specific payment strategies, and explore creative financing options to help ensure you and your clients are winning in this market. I would love to learn more about your business and explore ways we can support you. Would you be open to a quick 20-minute meeting sometime next week?"
When scheduling — offer two options:
"Would Tuesday at 11:00 or Thursday at 2:00 work better for you?"
Cold Call — Common Responses
What's the meeting about?"We'd love to learn more about the clients and areas you serve, what you're currently focused on growing, and where additional lending support may be helpful. We can also share some of the financing strategies and listing resources we provide to our agent partners."
Already have a lender"That's completely understandable, and we're not asking you to replace a relationship that's working well. We'd simply love the opportunity to introduce ourselves and become an additional resource when you need a second opinion, have a challenging scenario, or need extra support. Would you still be open to a 20-minute meeting?"
Send me some info"Absolutely. I want to make sure I send something relevant. Would buyer financing options, listing-specific payment strategies, or solutions for more challenging scenarios be most helpful? I'll send that over today. Would it be okay if I follow up with you on [day]?"
I'm busy right now"No problem at all, I completely understand. Would there be a better time for me to call you back, or would it be easier if I sent you a quick text?"
Not interested"I completely understand. Thank you for taking my call. I'll send you my contact information in case you ever need an additional lending resource or a second opinion on a scenario."
No Answer — Follow-Up Text
Send immediately after the missed call.
"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM Lending. I just tried to give you a quick call to introduce myself. We love working alongside agents to support their buyers, create listing-specific payment strategies, and explore creative financing options to help you and your clients win in this market. I would love to connect for a quick 20-minute conversation. Do you have any availability next week?"

Gifting Campaign Scripts (4-Week Cadence)
Always try a call first. If no answer, follow up with a text.
Week 1: Break the Ice
Call:
"Hey [Agent Name], this is [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM Lending. I wanted to follow up after our meeting. It was great connecting with you and I'm excited to work together. How has your week been going?"
Text if no answer:
"Hey [Agent Name], this is [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM. Great meeting with you. Looking forward to working together. Here to support you and your buyers."
Week 2: Value Touch
Call:
"Hey [Agent Name], it's [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM. I've been thinking about your business and wanted to share something I think will be useful for you. [share resource or market update]. Would you have a few minutes this week to connect?"
Text if no answer:
"Hey [Agent Name], it's [Your Name] with NFM. I have something I think will be useful for you. [resource]. Let's connect this week!"
Week 3: Opportunity Call
Call:
"Hey [Agent Name], it's [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM. I've really enjoyed getting to know you. Do you have any buyers right now we can help with? We would love to earn your business."
Text if no answer:
"Hey [Agent Name], it's [Your Name] with NFM. Do you have any buyers we can help with right now? We would love to earn your business."
Week 4: Accountability Follow-Up
Call:
"Hey [Agent Name], it's [Your Name] with the Daniel Sa Team at NFM. Wanted to connect and see how your business is going. Do you have any buyers or listings we can support? We are committed to being your go-to team and we are here whenever you need us."
Text if no answer:
"Hey [Agent Name], it's [Your Name] with NFM. Do you have any buyers or listings we can support right now? We are here whenever you need us."
Note: Replace [resource or market update] in Week 2 with something specific to that agent. The more relevant it is to their market or situation, the better it lands.
New Agent Meeting — Commitment & 4-Week Follow-Up
The most common failure mode: great meeting, agent signs up for the mailing list, nothing else happens. This framework closes that gap. The commitment ask is the single most important moment — get it every time, no exceptions.
The Commitment Ask — end of every new agent meeting
"Do I have your commitment to send me the next two to three leads that come your way?"
Deliver this while looking them in the eye and shaking their hand. This creates a spoken agreement that is genuinely difficult to break without consciously going back on their word. Ending with a vague "let me know if I can ever help" puts zero pressure on the agent and gives you no leverage to follow up on. Getting a lead within the first 30 days is critical — after that window closes, the agent has usually reverted to their previous lender and reconverting them becomes exponentially harder.
The 4-Week Follow-Up Structure
Week 0 — Day of the meeting
Send the How to Refer template. Add them on social media. No ask yet — pure value delivery.
Week 1 — Break the walls down
Send a relatable meme or short video — nothing salesy. Just staying on their radar in a human way.
No direct ask. Goal is to make them smile and remember you.
Week 2 — Value touch, still no ask
Send something relevant — market update, sales meeting content, or a financing strategy tip. Nothing that asks for a lead.
Patience here pays off in week 3. Two touches, no pressure — this is what separates you from the LO who emailed three times in a row asking for business.
Week 3 — First lead ask, paired with a unique product
"Hey [Agent], I was thinking about you — do you have any buyers who've been told no elsewhere? We have a 1099 program / bank statement option / portfolio loan that might be worth a look. Also, did anyone come to mind since we met that I could help with?"
Lead with the unfamiliar product, not a well-known one. Every agent has heard of bank statement and DSCR — leading with something more unique (1099, portfolio program) signals you have tools others don't and gives them a specific reason to think of a client.
Week 4 — Accountability call
"Hey [Agent], I wanted to circle back — when we met, you committed to sending me the next couple of leads that came your way. Is there anyone you're working with right now I could help with?"
If they push back or go quiet:
"Did I do something wrong?"
This surfaces the real reason almost every time — fix it, or pivot: "Would you be open to just introducing me to whoever you're currently working with? I'd love the chance to earn a second opinion."

For Existing Partners — shifting to earning 100% of their business
"What's one thing we could do better to make working together easier for you?"
Invites specific, actionable feedback and deepens trust simultaneously. The agent who answers this honestly is the one you'll earn 100% of business from.
Pre-Approval Pipeline Review (monthly or bi-weekly to top agents)
Send the agent a view of their entire pre-approved pipeline with current status. Creates mutual accountability — they see their buyers' statuses, it surfaces where leads are being lost, and it makes you look like a partner, not a vendor.

ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE APPROACH · EVERY AGENT CONVERSATION

Make every agent conversation count.

Not just “Hi, how are you?” Prepare with the pipeline, discuss each buyer with the agent, uncover opportunities and close the loop.

Before → During → After

Before: Open the existing authorized pipeline and review recent leads, pre-approvals, and contracts.

During: Talk through each buyer with the agent. Ask what they've heard, how LP communication has been and what we could improve.

After: Determine next action, verify LP involvement if necessary, update the agent and log meaningful relationship notes.

Questions to uncover opportunities

  • Lender rescue: Any unhappy buyers or deals going sideways with another lender?
  • Purchase power: Anyone needing a higher pre-approval? The LP can review eligibility.
  • Offer strategy: Buyers shopping but not winning? Discuss the 10 Ways to Win.
  • Cash to close: Buyers who need down-payment or closing-cost assistance? Explore NFM FLEX eligibility with the LP.
  • Contingencies: Buyers who need to sell first? Ask the LP about bridge or equity options.

Close the loop—every time

  1. Amanda identifies the concern or opportunity.
  2. She handles what she can, or assigns the right LP.
  3. When an LP is involved, she verifies what they actually did.
  4. She contacts the agent again, communicates the outcome and asks whether everything is good.
  5. She records agent feedback and keeps unresolved follow-ups open until complete.
Important boundaries

Amanda owns the agent relationship and updates. LPs own buyer conversations and financing execution. Do not enter buyer names, financial details or duplicate pipeline records into the RM Tracker. Product solutions are subject to eligibility.
